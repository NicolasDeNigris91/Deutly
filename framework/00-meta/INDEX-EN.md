## Stage 1 — FUNDAMENTE (Foundational Structures, A1→A2 structural)

> **Expected output:** you syntactically decompose any German declarative sentence, justify each Kasus / Genus / Numerus / Konjugation, and produce simple sentences without structural errors.

| ID | Module | Prereqs | Output |
|----|--------|---------|--------|

| 01-03 | Verbal System I — Tense, Conjugation, Separability | 01-01 | Conjugates strong + weak verbs in all indicative tenses. |
| 01-04 | Nominal Inflection — Genus, Numerus, Adjective Declension | 01-02 | Applies weak/strong/mixed adjective declension without hesitation. |
| 01-05 | Pronominal System | 01-02, 01-04 | Manipulates Personal-, Reflexive-, Possessive-, Demonstrative-, Relative-Pronouns. |
| 01-06 | Word Formation I — Composition + Derivation | 01-04 | Decomposes ad hoc compounds; creates grammatical compounds. |

## Stage 6 — SPECIALIZATION TRACKS (post-CAPSTONE-5, optional)

> **Expected output:** you are a professional translator (Track A), academic doctoral student (Track B), specialist in legal/medical/technical German (Track C), or DaF teacher/mentor (Track D).

### Track A — Translation Studies + Practice

6 modules + CAPSTONE-6-A: Translation Theory deepened + Literary + Philosophical + Legal + Cultural Translation + Editorial work + Published book translation.

### Track B — Germanic Studies Research (Doctorate Preparation)

7 modules + CAPSTONE-6-B: Research question + Specialized academic writing + Conference practice + Academic networking + Doctoral application + Third-party funding + Habilitation + Doctorate begin.

### Track C — Specialized Technical Languages (3 sub-tracks)

- **C1 Legal German** (4 modules + CAPSTONE-6-C1): BGB/ZPO/StGB + Court Verdicts + Contract Law + Lawyer Writing.
- **C2 Medical German** (3 modules + CAPSTONE-6-C2): Anatomy + Diagnostic Reports + Medical Research Publication.
- **C3 Technical German** (3 modules + CAPSTONE-6-C3): DIN Standards + Patents + Technical Documentation.

### Track D — Mentoring + DaF Teaching

6 modules + CAPSTONE-6-D: DaF Methodology + CEFR Levels + Phonetics Teaching + Materials + DaF Diploma + Institutional Mentoring + Own DaF Practice (30+ learners).

## How to consult this framework in English

This translation is intended for:

1. **International learners (C1+ in German)** who are non-PT-BR speakers and wish to use FATHOM-Deutsch.
2. **Comparative L2-acquisition researchers** examining the framework structurally.
3. **DaF teachers** worldwide considering FATHOM-Deutsch as supplementary material.

**What this translation is NOT:**

- Complete translation of all modules + appendices + templates (planned for v4.0).
- Substitute for the PT-BR original — in case of inconsistencies, PT-BR is reference.
- Translation of practical examples in modules — German Belege (citations) remain in German; explanations in PT-BR.

**Sequence of multilingual expansion (planned):**

- v3.0: INDEX-EN (this file) + README-EN expanded + INDEX-ES + INDEX-FR brief intros.
- v3.5+: complete Stage 1-2 modules in EN.
- v4.0+: complete framework in EN.
